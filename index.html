SMART GOVERNANCE PROJECT CONCEPT

PROXIMA / LA-EWS

Land Acquisition Delay Early-Warning System (SIH Concept)

ℹ️ Note: This is an architectural proposal & concept for national hackathons/smart governance, designed to predict administrative bottlenecks.

Core Concept

Designed to predict potential land acquisition delays 45–90 days in advance by monitoring procedural milestones across departments, flagging risks before project schedules slip.

Risk Levels & Alert Hierarchy

LOW

On track. Procedural milestones met within expected SLA.

MEDIUM

Minor delay in inter-department survey or filing.

HIGH

Bottleneck detected; potential 30+ day delay forecast.

CRITICAL

Imminent 45–90 day stall; automated escalation to directors.

Target Stakeholders / Users

DRO (District Revenue Officer) CALA (Competent Authority for Land Acquisition) Tahsildars Project Directors

Key Features & Technical Vision

  • Early Warning System: Predictive notifications alerting officers to high-risk bottlenecks.
  • GIS Interactive Dashboard: Spatial visualization of project parcels and acquisition phases.
  • Multilingual Accessibility: Seamless localized interface in English, Tamil, and Hindi.
  • Predictive Analytics: Pattern analysis on legal objections, valuation delays, and payout timelines.
